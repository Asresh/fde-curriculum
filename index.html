THE FIELD METHOD

Learn by moving
one workflow forward.

Carry the same customer problem through the course. Each step adds a concrete decision or working artifact until you can explain what changed and how to operate it.

01 / UNDERSTANDStart with the people doing the workMap the current process, its exceptions, the systems involved, and the result that would matter.
02 / DELIVERBuild a narrow, usable sliceConnect representative data, make the boundaries explicit, and leave a safe path for human judgment.
03 / IMPROVEShow evidence and hand overRecord what worked, what did not, who owns operations, and what the next decision should be.

Use synthetic information for practice. Use real customer data only with explicit authorization.
